    Touch screens

    On a phone or tablet the game shows on-screen controls for player 1: put your thumb anywhere in the lower left and drag to steer, and use the buttons on the right. Slide your thumb from one button to another to switch. Auto A at the top keeps A held so you accelerate without holding a thumb on it; tap it again to stop. ☰ brings up the page buttons (this panel, Fullscreen, the game's Settings). Turn the device sideways for the biggest picture.

    Rebinding

    F10 (or the Settings button) opens the game's settings bar: key and button mapping, which pad is which player, vibration. Changes are saved in this browser.

    Page shortcuts

    ? or H toggles this panel, Esc closes it. Use the Fullscreen button for fullscreen.
